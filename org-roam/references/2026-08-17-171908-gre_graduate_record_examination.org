* Test Structure & Scoring
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       b5f66d7a-f34b-4257-8623-4e9c59e34155
:END:
+ Analytical Writing: One 30-minute "Analyze an Issue" task scored on a 0–6 scale.
+ Verbal Reasoning: Two sections assessing reading comprehension and vocabulary, scored on a 130–170 scale.
+ Quantitative Reasoning: Two sections testing basic math, algebra, geometry, and data analysis, scored on a 130–170 scale.
