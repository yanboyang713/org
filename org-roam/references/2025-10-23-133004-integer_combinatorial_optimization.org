#+title: Combinatorial Optimization
#+date: 2025-10-23

* Overview
Combinatorial optimization studies problems whose feasible solutions come from a finite or discrete set. The goal is to find the best solution according to an objective, such as the shortest route, a feasible schedule, or an optimal selection of projects.

Many such problems can be modeled using [[file:2026-10-29-integer_programming.org][integer programming (IP)]].

* Methods
** Exact methods
- *Branch and bound:* an exact method that divides a problem into smaller subproblems and uses bounds to rule out regions that cannot contain an optimal solution.
- *Dynamic programming:* solves a problem by breaking it into smaller, related subproblems and reusing their solutions.

** Heuristics and metaheuristics
Heuristics seek good solutions efficiently; metaheuristics provide general strategies for exploring large search spaces. They do not generally guarantee an optimal solution.

- *Genetic algorithm (GA):* evolves a population of candidate solutions using selection and variation.
- *Simulated annealing (SA):* explores solutions while gradually reducing its willingness to accept worse moves.
- *Particle swarm optimization (PSO):* updates a population of candidate solutions using information from each candidate and the swarm.
- *Ant colony optimization (ACO):* builds solutions using simulated pheromone trails that reinforce promising choices.
